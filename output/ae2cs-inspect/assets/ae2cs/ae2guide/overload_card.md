# Meteorite Overclock Card

<ItemImage id="ae2cs:overload_card" scale="2" />

The **Meteorite Overclock Card** accelerates supported AECS machines. Up to **2 cards** can be installed:

- **1 card:** an operation takes at most **4 game ticks**.
- **2 cards:** an operation takes at most **1 game tick**.

It can be installed in:

- <ItemLink id="ae2cs:crystal_growth_chamber" />
- <ItemLink id="ae2cs:crystal_vibration_chamber" />
- <ItemLink id="ae2cs:circuit_etcher" />
- <ItemLink id="ae2cs:crystal_pulverizer" />
- <ItemLink id="ae2cs:crystal_aggregator" />
- <ItemLink id="ae2cs:entropy_variation_reaction_chamber" />
- <ItemLink id="ae2cs:meteorite_pattern_provider" /> and its part form

Machines that consume AE power still require enough energy to reach the listed speed.

Speed Cards do not provide additional acceleration while a Meteorite Overclock Card is installed.

A Meteorite Pattern Provider can install up to **4 cards**. Each card adds **128 crafting requests** to its per-round parallel capacity:

- **1 card:** **+128** requests per round.
- **2 cards:** **+256** requests per round.
- **3 cards:** **+384** requests per round.
- **4 cards:** **+512** requests per round.
